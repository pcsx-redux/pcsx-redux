pcsx-redux

The PlayStation emulator and debugger, running in your browser. Discs and executables you load are read locally and never uploaded. More ps1dev tools.

Needs a browser with WebGL 2 and SharedArrayBuffer: a current Chrome, Edge or Firefox. The first visit reloads the page once to enable the latter.

or drop the files on the page (a .cue with its .bin files) Then Emulation → Start.
